# Machina Labs

> Machina Labs builds software-defined factories for metal structures. Its RoboCraftsman
> platform pairs 7-axis robots with closed-loop AI control to roboform, scan, trim, drill
> and finish large sheet-metal parts without custom tooling, for aerospace, defense,
> hypersonics and automotive customers.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- Machina Labs publishes **no public API**. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL,
  MCP server, A2A agent card, SDK, CLI, webhook catalog or developer portal.
- Contract discovery was run against machinalabs.ai and docs.machinalabs.ai — /openapi.json,
  /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc, /graphql and
  every /.well-known/ path — and all returned HTTP 404.
- The company's capabilities page states parts "can integrate into existing PLM or MES
  systems through secure APIs." That is a capability sold through sales and applications
  engineering, not a self-serve developer program; no reference for it is published.
- Work with Machina Labs starts with a CAD model and a conversation, not an API key.

## Manufacturing envelope (from the capabilities page)

- Max part size: 12 ft (3.7 m) long, 5 ft (1.5 m) deep
- Max sheet thickness: 1/4 in (6.35 mm)
- Tolerance: +/-0.3% of maximum dimension, or +/-1 mm (0.04 in)
- Surface finish: 125 uin (3.2 um) Ra or lower
- Materials: aluminum, steels, stainless steels, titanium, nickel-based superalloys,
  refractory alloys, specialty composites
- Accepted input formats: STEP, IGES, STL
